02

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser so it can recognize you on the next page or the next visit. We also use a few close relatives — local storage, pixels in email, and software development kits — and this policy treats them all the same way.

Cookies set by plenary.life are first-party. Cookies set by a provider we work with, such as an analytics service, are third-party, and that provider can see them too. Every third-party cookie we use is listed in section 03 and in the Privacy policy.

04

Every way to opt out

  • The preference center above. Turn off any optional category and save. The choice is stored in an essential cookie on this browser, so repeat it on each browser or device you use. To change it later, come back to this page or open [Settings → Privacy] in the Service.
  • Global Privacy Control. If your browser or an extension sends the GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out of sale, sharing, and targeted advertising, and we turn marketing cookies off for you. You do not need to do anything else.
  • Your browser. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, for all sites or for plenary.life alone. Blocking essential cookies will sign you out and may stop the Service from working.
  • Provider opt-outs. [Analytics provider]: [opt-out link]. [Ad or attribution provider]: [opt-out link]. Industry tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance (optout.aboutads.info) and, in Europe, Your Online Choices (youronlinechoices.eu) also apply.
  • Email. Marketing email carries an unsubscribe link, and we stop within ten business days. Images in our email may include a pixel that tells us the message was opened; blocking images in your mail client disables it. Radar alerts and receipts are part of the Service and continue while you have an account; tune Radar in Settings.
  • Do not sell or share. We do not sell personal information. The switch above records your opt-out under US state privacy law and turns off marketing cookies. Residents of California and other states can also make the request by email — see the Privacy policy, section 13.

Opting out never changes the price, quality, or availability of the Service.
